# Changelog

All notable changes to RoofRoof are documented here.

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Platform imported from SQMeter 877208b and stripped to what a roof controller needs (spec 001).
- Safety inputs: the weather verdict from SQMeter (HTTP and/or MQTT) and the rain contact, and the park confirmation from N.I.N.A.'s Advanced API and the park switches; logged on change, set through the settings API (spec 004).
- Close on unsafe weather: when the weather isn't safe and the roof isn't closed, the controller asks N.I.N.A. to park, waits for the confirmed park and closes; every step has a time limit, a person's stop is respected, and the critical failures go out as `roof` alerts; new setting `park.timeoutSeconds` (spec 005).
- Roof alerts: seven roof events in Settings → Alerts with their own level, sound and wording: the critical "mount didn't park" and "roof didn't close" (never below urgent, never paused or switched off, sent again until a channel takes them), and a roof fault, a low 12 V supply, a lost weather source, the weather closing the roof and the roof closed afterwards. The `roof` alert event is replaced by `mount_not_parked` and `roof_not_closed` (spec 007).
- ASCOM Alpaca Dome: the roof as a Dome with a shutter (open, close, abort, shutter status, slewing) for N.I.N.A. and other clients, through the same safety checks as the web UI; a client can't move a roof whose position is unknown or clear a fault, and its stop never stands the automatic close down; ConformU runs in CI against the simulator's Dome (spec 006). If Alpaca was already on, imaging apps can open, close and stop the roof as soon as this release is installed (within the safety rules); turn Alpaca off under Settings → Network if you don't want that.
- Data interfaces: the roof's state as one retained MQTT document on `<base>/state` (the same roof object as `/api/status`), Home Assistant discovery of a Roof cover with position, supply, Hall and (with current sense) motor current sensors and a fault sensor, and the automatic close's phase and the weather and park checks in the status. New setting `mqtt.roofCommands` (off by default) accepts OPEN, CLOSE and STOP on `<base>/roof/set`, with an Alpaca client's limits: no move from an unknown position, no fault clearing, no stopping or reversing the automatic close in bad weather, retained messages ignored; every command is answered on `<base>/roof/result` (spec 008).
- The demo has a roof: the firmware's own motion logic, command checks, close-on-unsafe sequence and roof alerts drive a simulated motor, magnets and Hall sensor; the Alpaca Dome is listed; the Demo panel can make the weather unsafe or rainy, unpark the mount, make the park fail, jam the roof and drop the 12 V supply (spec 011).
- Bench mode: a separate firmware build (`pio run -e esp32dev_bench`) for a bare board, with a simulated roof and the motor driver's pins held low, to try the web UI, MQTT and the Alpaca Dome (and ConformU) without a roof. The standard firmware refuses a bench image over the web upload and command-line OTA; a bench board that sees a 12 V supply latches the fault `benchSupply` instead of simulating; the status's new `roof.simulation` field, a banner on every page, the Dome's description and "Bench:" alert titles say so (spec 011).
- The platform while the roof moves: every flash write (settings, restores, saved values, the roof's fault record) counts as busy, so an open waits and a close waits for it; checks and moves are decided under one lock, so they can't overlap; restarts, firmware uploads and WiFi changes wait for the automatic close; the setup hotspot can't open the roof; MQTT settings are applied on the main loop; the start-up log names the reset reason (spec 010).
- Dashboard and settings: a Roof card with the state, the position, Open, Close and Stop (Stop never disabled; Open and Close greyed with the reason, and never offered without live data), the fault with Clear fault, the weather and park checks, the readings and the live Hall reading against its thresholds; a Stop bar on every page while the roof moves; roof faults and a stuck automatic close on the Status card; and a Roof tab in Settings for the motion, Hall, current and supply settings, the weather sources and the mount park, including the park time limit (spec 009).
- Daylight failsafe: a roof left open after sunrise is no longer silent. New setting `daylight.mode` (`off`, `alert` or `close`, default `alert`, so an update never starts closing the roof) and `daylight.sunAltitudeDeg` (default 0, -18 to 30), with the site in `location.set`, `location.latitude` and `location.longitude`; the sun's altitude is worked out on the device. With **Alert** the new events "Roof open in daylight", "Roof closed after daylight" and "Daylight check not working" (`daylight_open`, `daylight_closed`, `daylight_unknown`) say so and nothing moves. With **Alert and close** the roof is parked and closed by spec 005's sequence, with its critical alerts worded for daylight; opening is refused (`daylight`) and an open under way is stopped while the sun is up, a client's stop of that close is refused, and a person's Stop stands it down while the sun is up. Unsafe weather is unchanged: it takes over a daylight close at once (after 30 s of unsafe weather in all when a person stopped it or it was halted). After the update, a device without its site set sends "Daylight check not working: Location not set" once, 10 minutes after the restart. The status has `roof.daylight`, the Roof card a Daylight line and Settings → Roof a Daylight card, and the demo a "Sun up" switch (spec 013).
- Setup wizard: once the device has joined your WiFi, ten pages walk through the essential settings (device name and password, site and time, the hardware with live supply, park switch and rain contact readings, the Hall sensor with its live scale, travel, speed and a 40 % first-run top duty, the weather sources and N.I.N.A. each with a Test, the alert channel with a test, daylight) and end with a review of every check; it saves through the settings API (refused while the roof moves) and never moves the roof. New settings `setup.state` (`new`, `offered`, `done`) and `setup.step`: a new device opens the wizard, a device upgraded with settings already in place gets a dismissible banner instead, and **System → Setup** runs it again. New read-only `GET /api/roof/inputs` (the weather sources, the park and N.I.N.A.'s last reading). The demo restarts it from its panel (spec 015).
